# Completed Submissions are fully immutable

The spec both says work notes stay editable and that a Completed Submission is not editable for anyone. Work notes are the sole exception to the post-submit lock while a Submission is Submitted or Reviewed; PM and CoE Head otherwise act only through their gate actions and may not rewrite the Member's submitted content. Completed is fully locked, including work notes, because a journal that stays open after the CoE Head gate would be a back channel around the terminal state.
